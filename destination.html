Destination Information

Explore more about the popular tourist destinations of Sri Lanka.

Sigiriya

Sigiriya is an ancient rock fortress located in the Matale District. It is famous for its historical importance, gardens and paintings.

Ella

Ella is a beautiful destination in the hill country. It is popular for its mountains, waterfalls and scenic railway views.

Galle Fort

Galle Fort is a historical attraction located in southern Sri Lanka. It is famous for its colonial architecture and coastal beauty.

Mirissa

Mirissa is a popular coastal destination in southern Sri Lanka. It is known for its beautiful beaches and ocean views.

Nuwara Eliya

Nuwara Eliya is known for its cool climate, tea plantations and beautiful green hills.

Yala National Park

Yala National Park is famous for its wildlife, natural landscapes and biodiversity.

Anuradhapura

Anuradhapura is an ancient city famous for its historical monuments and religious places.

Kandy

Kandy is a cultural city famous for its traditions and the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic.
